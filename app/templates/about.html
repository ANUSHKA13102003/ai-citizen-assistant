AI Citizen Assistant: Retrieval-Augmented Generation & Personal Document Intelligence

Abstract

Indian citizens frequently interact with complex personal records—marksheets, resumes, income/caste certificates—alongside multi-page public scholarship and welfare scheme guidelines. General-purpose chatbots often hallucinate eligibility criteria or lose track of file provenance. AI Citizen Assistant implements a privacy-conscious Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipeline: PDFs are parsed page by page with pypdf (extended with pdfplumber tables and optional Tesseract OCR), segmented via LangChain's RecursiveCharacterTextSplitter (500 characters, 50 overlap) with (filename, page) metadata, embedded locally using all-MiniLM-L6-v2 (384-d), and indexed inside isolated per-session ChromaDB collections (citizen_documents). Mode 1 generates a standardized 7-section profile summary, while Mode 2 answers grounded queries over the last 6 conversation turns with exact file+page citations, a fixed refusal fallback, and a deterministic Python scheme eligibility engine.

System Architecture

Limitations & Future Work

  • Scanned regional-language PDFs require Tesseract language packs installed in the runtime environment.
  • Live generative reasoning transmits retrieved snippets to the hosted Gemini API; future deployments may integrate on-premise quantized models.
  • Complex multi-condition state-specific domicile rules can be expanded via declarative YAML rule packs.
