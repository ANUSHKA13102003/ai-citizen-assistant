Privacy, Consent & Data Governance

1. External LLM Transmission Disclosure (Paper Section III-I)

While PDF text extraction, chunking, and all-MiniLM-L6-v2 vector embeddings run locally on the application server, generating natural-language summaries (Mode 1), grounded Q&A responses (Mode 2), and advisory eligibility explanations sends your retrieved text chunks and question to the hosted Google Gemini API. Explicit consent is required before your first document upload.

2. Zero Document Storage on Disk or Database

Uploaded PDF bytes, extracted page text, and ChromaDB embeddings reside strictly in ephemeral RAM scoped to your isolated session ID. Our SQLite database logs only aggregate operational counts, question categories, latency measurements, and thumbs up/down feedback—never file contents or raw personal data.

3. Session Auto-Expiry & Instant Deletion

Sessions automatically expire after 60 minutes of inactivity (configurable via SESSION_TTL_MINUTES), purging the corresponding ChromaDB collection. You may also click Delete My Data Now inside the Workspace at any moment to immediately erase your vectors, extracted text, and conversation history.
